Why I’m here

I care deeply about the planet and the animals we share it with, as well as protecting the places that need it most, and I want to see those protections upheld and restored where they’ve been lost. I’m detail-oriented, I enjoy working on a team and learning from them as we go, and I want my work to genuinely help people.

Landscape architecture is where all of that can come together. It’s exciting to dream about designing outdoor parks, reserves, and even restoration projects in coastal, forested, and alpine environments. And if we can create inclusive, sustainable spaces that empower people to feel more connected to and invested in the world around them, that’s the goal.
